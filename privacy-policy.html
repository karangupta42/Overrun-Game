OVERRUN

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Short version: OVERRUN does not require an account and does not collect personal data. Your progress is saved only on your own device. The only third-party data collection possible is through optional rewarded video ads, which is handled by Google AdMob.

1. No Account Required

OVERRUN does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play. You can install and play the full game without ever entering your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying detail.

2. No Personal Data Collected

We (the developer of OVERRUN) do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from players. We have no servers that receive your gameplay data, no analytics SDKs tracking your behavior, and no way to identify you as an individual player.

3. Local-Only Save Data

Your progress — unlocked raiders, stars, level completion, Armory upgrades, salvage, and settings — is stored only locally on your device using standard platform save storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not backed up to any server we control
  • Is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data

4. Optional Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob)

OVERRUN may offer optional rewarded video ads (for example, a 2× salvage bonus or a continue after losing a run). These ads are entirely optional — declining them never blocks gameplay. When shown, rewarded ads are served through Google AdMob. Per Google's own policies, AdMob may collect an advertising identifier (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA) and other device information to serve and measure ads.

WhatWhoPurpose
Advertising ID / IDFA, device & ad-interaction dataGoogle AdMobServe and measure optional rewarded ads

This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy and data policies, not by us directly. You can review how Google handles this data here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob apps

Where required by your device's platform, you can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier from your device's system settings.

5. Children's Privacy

OVERRUN does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app requires no account and no personal data entry, no such information is ever requested from players of any age. If ads are shown, ad configuration follows applicable child-directed advertising requirements (such as Google's Ad Content Filtering and, where relevant, COPPA-compliant ad settings).

6. Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any player data, because we do not collect any beyond what is described above (which flows to Google AdMob only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad).

7. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes — for example, if new features add analytics or additional ad networks — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the game can be sent to: karangupta42@gmail.com
